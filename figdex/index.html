figdex

A figure collection showcase app. Record the figures you own, and display them in showcases for other collectors to see.

What you can do

  • Keep a record of your collection: purchase details, condition, photos, and notes.
  • Arrange figures in showcases and share them.
  • Browse a shared catalog, and follow other collectors.
  • Make a 3D model of a figure from photos taken from many angles.

Signing in

You can sign in with a Kakao or Google account. The details of what figdex collects and why are in the privacy policy.
