What this site collects

This site has no accounts, forms or comments, so there is nothing for you to submit. It remembers one thing in your own browser: the language you chose on the homepage (a site-language entry in local storage). That value never leaves your device.

The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes technical request data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver pages and protect against abuse, and shows us visit counts in aggregate.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense on some of our sites. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, and read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
