catchmeif404 is the name behind a small set of products we design, build and run ourselves. The name comes from the HTTP status code: a 404 is what you get when something is not where you looked, and we like making the thing that should have been there.

What we make

  • figdex is a mobile app for cataloguing and displaying a figure collection: shelves you can turn around in 3D, a dex that shows which pieces of a line you still miss, and showcases other collectors can visit. It is in closed testing on Android.
  • Maskman is a one-on-one stick-figure fighting game that runs in the browser, with online matches between two devices and a roguelike adventure mode. Play it at maskman.catchmeif404.com.
  • SantaPick is a Christmas site for friends: a Secret Santa draw with one shared link and no sign-up, a room your friends decorate for you, and a small village to walk around together. It lives at santapick.catchmeif404.com.
  • nightshift is an open-source runner for coding agents: tickets go in, one model builds, another reviews, and a person decides only what a person has to. The code is on GitHub under the MIT licence.

Why there are notes here

Most of what we learn comes from something going wrong in production or from a design choice that looked small and was not. We write those down in Notes: what the problem was, what we tried, what we kept. They are written from the code and the deploy records of the products above, so the numbers in them are ours and the mistakes are too.

How we work

Everything ships in small versions. A release is a tagged version, not whatever was last pushed, and after each deploy we check the live service instead of trusting a green build. Products are available in Korean and English where it makes sense for the people using them.
